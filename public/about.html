About Hi Temp Mail

What is Hi Temp Mail?

Hi Temp Mail is a free, privacy-first disposable email service that gives you a working temporary email address the moment you open the app — no account, no signup, no personal details. Use it to receive verification emails, one-time passwords (OTPs), download links and newsletters without ever exposing your real inbox.

Who is it for?

Anyone who needs a quick email address for a low-risk signup: free trials, public Wi-Fi portals, forum accounts, one-time downloads, app testing, or simply receiving a verification code without sharing your primary email. If you would not write your real address on a sticky note in a café, use a temporary one.

How it works

  1. 1 Open the app — a secure mailbox is generated instantly.
  2. 2 Copy the address and paste it into any signup or verification form.
  3. 3 Watch the inbox — verification codes are detected automatically and shown as a one-tap copy chip.

Feature highlights

  • check Instant mailbox generation — an address is ready in seconds

  • check Saved Mailboxes — bookmark a mailbox and restore it days later; OTPs keep arriving
  • check OTP auto-detection with auto-copy option
  • check Attachments — view and download files from emails
  • check Privacy blur, remote-image blocking and sanitized email HTML
  • check Light & dark themes, pull-to-refresh, offline-friendly local storage

  • check No ads, no analytics, no tracking

Technology

Hi Temp Mail is a single-page web app that talks to the Mail.tm REST API through a lightweight technical relay (Mail.tm's API does not allow direct browser access from third-party sites). Your mailbox credentials live only in your browser's local storage. Hi Temp Mail is an independent project and is not affiliated with Mail.tm.
