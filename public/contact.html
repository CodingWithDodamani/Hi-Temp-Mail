Support is self-serve — by design

Hi Temp Mail keeps no accounts, no server database, no analytics and no tracking, so there is no support desk that can look up your mailbox — there is simply nothing to look up. Almost every question is already answered in depth: mailbox lifetime and the Extend button, OTP auto-detection and auto-copy, saving and restoring mailboxes days later, attachments, privacy blur, backup export and import, and what to do when a mailbox stops working. Start with the FAQ — it resolves the vast majority of issues in under a minute, without handing over any personal data.

Reach the maintainer

For bug reports, accessibility problems, security disclosures and business enquiries, write to contact@hitempmail.app. Include the page or screen where the problem happened, your browser and device, the exact steps to reproduce it, and what you expected instead. Screenshots help. Please never send mailbox passwords, tokens or message contents — support never needs them, and anything you send by email is outside the app's private-by-architecture boundary.

Response expectations

  • check Security reports are read first and acknowledged as soon as possible.
  • check Bug reports with reproduction steps are answered within a few days.
  • check Lost mailboxes cannot be recovered by anyone — if an address is gone from the provider, it is gone. Save important mailboxes early and export backups from Settings.
