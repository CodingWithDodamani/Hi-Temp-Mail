Frequently asked questions

What is a temporary email address?

A temporary (or disposable) email address is a real, working inbox that you use instead of your personal email when signing up somewhere. It receives mail exactly like a normal address, but it is not connected to your identity, so spam and data leaks never reach your real inbox.

How long does a mailbox last?

By default a mailbox lives for 10 minutes, and you can extend the timer with one tap. If you save a mailbox to your Saved Mailboxes vault, you can restore it any time — the same day or days later — and keep receiving mail, including verification codes.

Can I receive OTP and verification codes?

Yes. Hi Temp Mail automatically detects 4–8 digit codes in incoming mail and shows them as a highlighted chip in the inbox list and a large copy button in the message view. There is also an optional setting that copies the code to your clipboard the instant it arrives.

What does saving a mailbox do?

Saving stores the mailbox address and its password in your browser's local storage — nothing is sent to any server of ours. You can then reopen that exact mailbox later from the Saved tab and continue receiving mail addressed to it.

Do you read or store my emails?

No. The app runs entirely in your browser and has no server database. Messages are created and stored by the Mail.tm service when the app requests them on your behalf. There are no analytics scripts, no advertising and no tracking of any kind.

Is Hi Temp Mail free?

Yes — every feature is free, and there is no account, subscription or ad in the app.

Can I send email or attachments?

Hi Temp Mail is receive-only. You can view and download attachments that arrive in your mailbox, but sending email is not supported — which also means the app cannot be used for outbound spam.

Why did my email stop working?

Unsaved temporary mailboxes are discarded when you delete them or when the provider expires them. If a saved mailbox stops working, it was most likely removed on the provider side after prolonged inactivity; generate a fresh one or remove it from your saved list.

Is it safe to use for banking or important accounts?

No. Disposable addresses are for low-risk signups only — trials, downloads, forums, Wi-Fi portals. Never use one for banking, government, healthcare, work or any account whose loss would hurt you, because anyone with access to the mailbox could receive its password resets.

How do I delete everything?

Use Delete Mail in the app to remove the current mailbox from the provider, Clear all in Settings → Data to empty your saved vault, and clear your browser's site data to wipe every local trace. That is everything — there is nothing stored anywhere else.
