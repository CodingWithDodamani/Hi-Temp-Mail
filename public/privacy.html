Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2025

Overview

AR TEMPMAIL is private by architecture, not just by policy. The app has no user accounts, no server-side database of its own, no analytics and no advertising. This page explains exactly what data exists and where.

What we never collect

  • check Your real email address or identity
  • check Analytics, advertising IDs, fingerprints or tracking pixels
  • check Cookies used for tracking (the app sets none)
  • check Server-side logs of your mailbox contents (we keep none)

Data stored on your device

The app stores its data in your browser's local storage. It never leaves your device, because we operate no server that could receive it:

  • check theme — your light/dark preference
  • check ar_saved_mails — mailboxes you bookmarked: address, its Mail.tm password and timestamps, so they can be restored later
  • check ar_active_session — the current mailbox session, so reloading keeps the same address (can be turned off in Settings)
  • check ar_settings — your in-app preferences (auto-refresh interval, sound, vibration, auto-copy OTP, privacy blur)
  • check read / hidden message ids — so unread badges stay accurate

How to erase everything

Use Delete Mail for the current mailbox, Clear all (Settings → Data) for saved mailboxes, and your browser's 'clear site data' for a full wipe. After that, no record of your mailboxes exists anywhere except on the provider's servers until they expire the account.

Third-party mail storage (Mail.tm)

Temporary mailboxes and their messages are created and stored by the Mail.tm service (api.mail.tm) under Mail.tm's own terms and privacy policy — please review them on mail.tm. When you generate a mailbox, the app registers an account there using a random address and a random password. Because Mail.tm's API does not allow direct browser calls from third-party sites, your requests are transparently proxied through a technical relay on this site. The relay forwards your requests and responses as-is and stores nothing.

Emails and tracking pixels

Remote images inside emails are blocked by default, so senders cannot log your IP address through tracking pixels; you can load images for a specific message when you choose to. Email HTML is always sanitized before display: scripts, frames, forms and event handlers are removed, and links open with rel='noopener noreferrer' and a no-referrer policy.

Security

Mailbox passwords are generated with the browser's cryptographic random generator and stored only on your device. Access tokens are kept in memory, not written to storage, unless you enable Remember session, which stores the minimum needed to restore your own mailbox on reload. Email content is rendered through a strict sanitizer.

Children

AR TEMPMAIL is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect any personal information from anyone — there is simply nothing to collect.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will always be published on this page. Material changes will be summarized at the top.
